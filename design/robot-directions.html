BLUE & ORANGE · three design directions

Original robots, drawn in 2D before any 3D work (M3.1), in DEV's light and palette: flat matte facets, one warm key from camera-right, one blue rim from behind-left. BLUE is the eager one (small, round, one antenna); ORANGE is the deadpan one (taller, squarer, heavy lids, ears). Each direction changes what matters most: the shell shapes, how they move (hover, walk, roll) and how the face carries feeling (light on a screen, or ink on the shell). Pick one, or a mix (e.g. "A's bodies, C's faces").
