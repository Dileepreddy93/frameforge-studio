Rules

  • Shells are matte faceted superellipsoids: BLUE's head is a soft capsule, ORANGE's a flat-topped box. No gloss, no clearcoat, no emissive.
  • The face is flat light on a dark visor (screen glass #151029): BLUE in screen blue #79C5FF, ORANGE in window amber #FFC15A. Flat and unlit, like DEV's ink.
  • Eyes carry the mood with an upper-lid cut and its tilt; happy is an arch; ORANGE's resting lid sits a third down.
  • Joints, antenna ball, ear caps and thruster ring are the desk's violet. Both hover on a small thruster; hands are mittens (BLUE's float, ORANGE's are jointed).
  • Heights: BLUE 1.48 (head centre 1.0), ORANGE 1.58 (head centre 1.3); DEV is 2.0. BLUE's eyes sit at DEV's chin, ORANGE's at his mouth.
