The set · three directions

Drawn in 2D before any three.js work (M5.1), in STYLE.md's light and colours, with DEV, BLUE and ORANGE drawn by their approved model-sheet code at true scale. Every 9:16 panel is phone size. Target from the reference wides: the cast stand 9–21% of the frame height (ours today: 58%), a key is about 0.3 of a character's height, and the background is a wall of app windows. Pick one, or a mix (e.g. "A's laptop with C's reverse-angle screens").

Forms: design/set-forms.js; layouts and cameras: design/set-directions.js. The app windows are element lists, so the chosen layout's numbers and screens carry into the three.js build.
