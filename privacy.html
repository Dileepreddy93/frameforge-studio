Privacy

Draft, 9 November 2026. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, and will be before FrameForge takes payments.

FrameForge runs in your browser. There is no account, and your stories, voices and films stay on your own computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Your projects: saved in your browser's storage (IndexedDB, with a small copy in local storage so a refresh can't lose your last change), including up to five earlier versions. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them; use Download project to keep a copy.
  • Voices: every line is voiced on your computer by the Kokoro voice model. Your text is never sent anywhere.
  • Films: drawn and encoded on your computer. We never see them.
  • Problem reports: the Creator's Report a problem button builds a report you can read, copy or download. Nothing is sent unless you choose to post it yourself.

What other services see

Like any website, these see your IP address and browser when your browser asks them for files:

  • Vercel, which hosts this site, keeps standard request logs.
  • Hugging Face, the first time the Creator downloads the voice model (about 92 MB or 326 MB). After that, it's stored in your browser.
  • Google Fonts, for the landing page's fonts.
  • jsDelivr, only if one of our own copies of a library fails to load.

What we don't do

No cookies, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no sale of data. If we add simple, anonymous visit counts later, this page will say so first.

Children

FrameForge is not directed at children under 13.
